## About

I've worked on almost every layer of software: financial systems, marketing products, internal tools, ERP design, and now data and AI platforms. Somewhere along the way the job stopped being "write code" and became "make sure the right thing gets built, ships on time, and still works a year later."

- 🧠 **Architecture.** Data, AI and cloud setups that fit the business instead of fighting it.
- 🎨 **Product & UX.** Designing what users need, not what's easiest to build.
- 🛠️ **Engineering.** Hands-on full stack, from React frontends to Python and C# backends.
- 👥 **Team leadership.** Structuring teams so they own their work and like doing it.
- 🤝 **Translation.** Connecting technical and non-technical people so decisions actually get made.

> I care about building software that helps organizations grow, and teams that enjoy creating it.
